About

Who we are and what we do for Wayne County

Who we are

Coordinating the county's response

Wayne County EMA is established within County Government. We coordinate emergency and disaster mitigation, preparedness, planning, response, and recovery for the County and its political subdivisions, working with the State of Illinois, private organizations, businesses, and the public.

We also ensure the county stays in compliance with state and federal emergency management requirements, which maximizes preparedness and grant availability, enhances resident safety, and reduces property damage through mitigation.

Our history
  1. Civil DefenseThe agency was originally created as the county's Civil Defense organization.
  2. ESDAIt later became the Emergency Services and Disaster Agency.
  3. Wayne County EMARenamed Emergency Management Agency to more accurately reflect its responsibilities.
